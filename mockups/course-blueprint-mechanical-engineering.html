Mechanical Engineering

Mechanical Engineering is one of the widest branches of engineering, dealing with the design, production, and operation of machines. It applies principles of physics to design, analyse, and manufacture mechanical systems used across industries such as energy conversion, biotechnology, computers, electronics, and aerospace. More than 6,000 institutions in India offer this course, with strong graduate demand from automotive, aerospace, and industrial sectors.

🎓Eligibility & admission

Undergraduate (UG)

Candidates must complete 10+2 in the science stream with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as core subjects, scoring a minimum of 50% aggregate marks. Some relaxation exists for reserved category candidates. Diploma holders (3-year) in a relevant field can apply via Lateral Entry.

Postgraduate (PG)

Candidates must hold a B.E./B.Tech degree in Mechanical Engineering or a relevant discipline with at least 50% aggregate marks in final examinations; some institutes require 60%. Relaxation is available for reserved category candidates.

💼Career scope & job profiles

The scope of Mechanical Engineering is broad both in India and abroad, offering high-paying opportunities across aerospace, automobiles, chemical manufacturing, research, and oil exploration. With rapid technological advancement, graduates can also work in nanotechnology, biomedical engineering, artificial intelligence, robotics, and energy conservation. Graduates may also pursue higher studies such as M.Tech or Ph.D. for research and academic careers.

❓Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between B.E. and B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering?
A B.E. focuses on theoretical knowledge to help students invent new gadgets and contribute to technological innovation, while a B.Tech emphasises the practical application of engineering concepts in real-world scenarios and industry practices.
What is the fee range for Mechanical Engineering courses in India?
The average fees for Mechanical Engineering courses range from Rs. 3 lakhs to Rs. 15 lakhs for the entire duration, depending on the university, whether it's a government or private institute, and the level of study (UG or PG).
What is the duration of the B.Tech Mechanical Engineering course?
The B.Tech Mechanical Engineering course duration is four years, spread across eight semesters, covering core subjects, laboratory work, electives, and a final year project.
What are some popular Mechanical Engineering specializations?
Popular specializations include Thermal Engineering, Fluid Mechanics, Automation and Control, Materials Engineering and Processing, Machine Design, and Solid Mechanics, allowing students to focus on specific career paths within the field.
Why should I pursue an M.Tech in Mechanical Engineering?
An M.Tech provides deeper understanding of thermodynamics, solids, materials, fluid mechanics, and machine design, enhancing knowledge and unlocking senior-level career prospects across research, academia, and industry roles.
